# Code of conduct

Project spaces must remain professional, respectful and safe for participation.

Participants must not harass others, publish private information, pressure a
reporter to disclose business data, or use project access to contact people or
organizations outside the scope of a contribution.

Disagreement about design is expected. Discuss observable behavior, evidence,
trade-offs and project principles rather than personal motives. Maintainers may
edit, hide or remove content and temporarily or permanently restrict access when
needed to protect participants or private data.

Report conduct concerns privately to a maintainer. Do not include unrelated
credentials, tenant data or business content in a conduct report.
